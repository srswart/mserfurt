Your review—not an automatic score

For each letter, say keep or repair, and name the feature that matters:

  1. Identity: does it read naturally as the intended n, u or m?
  2. Slant and proportions: are the diagonal rises, descending stems and overall width appropriate?
  3. Turns and joins: are the arch/return shapes and lifted attachment points intentional-looking?
  4. Consistency: do all three look like constructions from the same stroke vocabulary?
