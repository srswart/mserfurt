## Proposed stroke-language abstraction

Unconnected control remains the exact canonical n and u, including their lift.
Connected nu is an optional contextual program:

1. Retain n's unaffected body and identify its declared terminal departure span.
2. Continue pen-down from that departure into **u.contextual-entry-rise**.
3. Follow with **u.entry-turn-and-descent**, joining the declared retained u body.
4. Retain u's lower return, subsequent rise and final stem unless a separately
   reviewed proposal explicitly changes their source intervals.

The entry rise belongs semantically to u, with n's departure recorded as a
contributor. The joint boundary owns compatibility constraints, not an extra
unowned bridge. Geometric pieces may remain split for numerical reasons; piece
count is not the definition of a gesture. Each span must retain source lineage,
directed ports, contact profile, pen-down run identity and excluded source spans.
Do not draw both the replaced prefix and its replacement.

Standalone u is unchanged. Connection selection is contextual, not a new global
u recipe. The proposal must say which features come from an existing validated
rise/turn gesture, which are adapted for this context, and why. Generic endpoint
interpolation with different labels does not demonstrate this abstraction.
