## Objective

Implement the interactive Pan-and-Zoom Manuscript Viewer (`/viewer`, `viewer.html`) and the 34-page Index of Leaves (`/folios`, `folios.html`) matching the desktop and phone artboards in `docs/site/MS Erfurt Aug. 12°47 — Edition & Manuscript.html`.

## Behavioral Change

After this advance:
- `/viewer` provides a full-viewport (`100vh`) dark inspection workspace supporting all 34 leaves (`f01r–f17v`) in **Single Leaf (`Leaf`)**, **Bound Bifolium (`Opening`)**, and **Clean vs. Weathered** modes, with pointer drag-to-pan, wheel/`±`/`Fit` zoom (`50%–500%`), mini-map navigator with viewport rectangle, 4 toggleable CLIO-7 overlay layers (`Lacunae & losses`, `Line confidence`, `Pressure analysis`, `Quill re-dips`), a 2-tab inspector (`Apparatus` & line-by-line FNHD `Transcription`), a 34-leaf bottom filmstrip with Roman numeral section dividers, and phone bottom-sheet support.
- `/folios` renders the complete 34-page Index of Leaves grouped by physical section runs with interactive filter chips (`All leaves 34`, `Water-damaged 5`, `Hand under strain 10`, `Offcut stock 8`) and status dots. The strain count is every leaf whose hand-change line count is at least 10.
